Elisa Terumi Rubel Schneider is a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Calgary, working with the Advanced Imaging and Artificial Intelligence Lab (AI2Lab). She holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Pontifical Catholic University of Paraná (PUCPR), Brazil, and an M.Sc. in Bioinformatics from the Federal University of Paraná (UFPR).

With over 15 years of experience in software engineering and applied artificial intelligence, her research focuses on Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, Generative AI, and multimodal machine learning. Her interests include information extraction, language model adaptation, and the development of AI methods for real-world applications.

At AI2Lab, her current research focuses on applying NLP and LLM to clinical data analysis. She investigates methods for extracting and structuring information from unstructured medical reports, processing and integrating clinical data, and supporting the analysis of patient outcomes.
